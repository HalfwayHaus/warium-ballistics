How this estimate works

Bombs use Futurism’s 0.2 block/tick release, 20-tick free-flight period, 0.05 steering factor and Minecraft arrow gravity/drag. Glide fins also offset horizontal drag. Powered missiles use their script’s initial velocity, acceleration threshold and powered tick count, then a gravity-only coast. The powered result assumes a straight level route at release altitude and is therefore an optimistic envelope.

Target acquisition, terrain, collisions, missile turning, launch orientation, ship motion and unloaded chunks are not simulated. IR, radar and optical weapons additionally require a valid lock. The loitering drone is pilot-controlled and has no fixed range from altitude alone.
