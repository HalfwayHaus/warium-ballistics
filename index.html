How the temporary map works

The calculator loads public map files as you explore and draws trajectories only in your temporary viewer. The original map is unchanged. Closing the viewer clears your overlay. Private maps that need a login are not supported. Map service instructions.

Calculation model

Motion: Minecraft 1.20.1 AbstractArrow order, with position advanced before 0.99 air drag and 0.05 downward velocity are applied each tick. Futurism railgun flight adds 0.04 upward velocity while moving at least 2 blocks/tick.

RPL: Warium doubles battle-cannon and artillery launch velocity when Ritchie's Projectile Library is loaded. Mortar launch speed remains 5 blocks/tick.

Mortars: The displayed mortar pitch is solved through Warium's actual transformed launch vector. A 0-degree mortar setting produces approximately 45 degrees of true elevation when fired on its centerline.

Error: Median and 90th-percentile miss at the vertical target plane use 800 deterministic trials of Minecraft's triangular projectile spread at the selected launch inaccuracy, including a second random perturbation for cluster bomblets. These are not horizontal ground-impact radii.

Coordinates: One block is treated as one meter. VS local coordinates must be converted to world coordinates.

Aimer limits: Yaw ±30.5°, pitch −21.5° to +44.5°. Out-of-range arcs require changed firing inputs and are not mapped. Control settings use 0.5° steps; charts show the exact nominal path. Terrain, collisions, vehicle motion and extra mount restrictions are not modeled.

Derived from Warium 1.3.2, Futurism 0.0.1 and Ritchie's Projectile Library 2.1.1 for Minecraft 1.20.1.
